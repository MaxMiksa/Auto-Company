## 3. Value Proposition Blocks

### Main Value Prop
**Compliance paperwork that feels like it was written for you — because it was.**

PolicyForge asks about your frameworks, stack, team size, and subprocessors, then generates a complete policy pack and control map that names the systems you actually use. No generic templates. No blank-page anxiety.

### Three Supporting Pillars

1. **Stack-aware, not boilerplate.**
   Your policies reference AWS, Google Workspace, Stripe, GitHub, Slack, and your other tools by name — so the output looks like your company wrote it.

2. **Control map included.**
   Every policy is mapped to the SOC 2 or ISO 27001 controls your auditor will test, making first-round review faster and cheaper.

3. **Start small, scale safely.**
   Try a free preview of one policy. Pay only when you download the full pack. Annual plans keep you current after the audit.

## 7. Trust and Objection Handlers

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| “Is this legal advice?” | No. PolicyForge generates a starting draft. Every document should be reviewed by your auditor, compliance consultant, or legal counsel before submission. |
| “Will my auditor accept AI-generated policies?” | The output is a professional first draft in standard policy language. You customize it to your practices and have it reviewed. |
| “How is this different from a template pack?” | Templates are static. PolicyForge asks about your real infrastructure and subprocessors, then maps each policy to the controls your auditor will test. |
| “What if my stack changes?” | Growth and Scale plans include annual reviews and redline updates. Starter customers can purchase a refresh. |
| “Do you store my data?” | We store only what’s required to generate and deliver your documents. You can delete your data at any time. We do not train third-party AI on your content. |
