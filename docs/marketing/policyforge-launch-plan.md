### Why this is cheaper — and better — than the alternatives

**Vs. a $10,000 consultant**  
Most compliance consultants sell you time, overhead, and liability. You still have to fill out their intake, and the output is often a dressed-up template. PolicyForge automates the intake-to-draft step, gives you editable files, and leaves the high-stakes advisory work to the Audit Assist tier — only if you need it. You pay for the deliverable, not the hand-holding.

**Vs. Vanta / Drata / Secureframe**  
Those platforms are powerful for continuous monitoring, evidence collection, and ongoing audits. Most seed-stage startups do not need continuous monitoring yet; they need policies and a control map to get through a first audit. PolicyForge is the on-ramp. It costs less than one month of the incumbents and produces files you can later import into them if you upgrade.

**The price signal**  
$349 is enough to signal serious value; $599/$1,199 annual tiers make the renewal decision obvious if the annual review saves even one hour of consultant time.

## 6. 5-Second Comprehension Test Protocol

### What the test measures

Within 5 seconds of viewing the hero section only, a visitor should be able to answer:

1. What is this product?
2. Who is it for?
3. What is the main benefit?
4. What should they do next?

### What a visitor must understand in 5 seconds

- **Product:** SOC 2 / ISO 27001 policy pack generator.
- **Audience:** Startups doing their first audit.
- **Benefit:** Tailored first draft in an afternoon, without a $10k consultant or $7.5k platform.
- **Next step:** Start a free preview.

### Test Protocol

1. **Recruit 5–8 target participants.** Ideal: seed/Series A founders, ops/finance leads, or compliance consultants who work with startups.
2. **Show the hero section for 5 seconds.** No scrolling, no clicking. Hide navigation and footer.
3. **Ask the 4 questions above** in a fixed order. Record exact answers.
4. **Score each answer** as:
   - 2 = correct and specific
   - 1 = close but vague
   - 0 = wrong or missing
5. **Pass criteria:**
   - ≥80% of participants score 2 on product and benefit.
   - ≥70% score 2 on audience and next step.
6. **If fail, iterate on one variable at a time:**
   - First adjust headline.
   - Then adjust subhead.
   - Then adjust CTA copy and visual hierarchy.
7. **Re-test with fresh participants.** Run until pass criteria are met before launch.

### 5-Second Copy Iteration Rules

- Headline must contain "SOC 2" or "ISO 27001" and a time or cost benefit.
- Subhead must answer "how?" in one line.
- CTA must be a verb + outcome with low friction: "Start free preview," not "Learn more."
- Remove any abstract adjectives like "revolutionary" or "seamless."
