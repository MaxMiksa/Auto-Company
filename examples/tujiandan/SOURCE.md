# Source and showcase changes / 来源与展示改动

图检单来自 Auto Company 的真实模型运行：原始记录共 5 个周期（3 个探索、2 个产品周期）。模型交付了本地图片批次检查和 CSV 交接工具，并根据当时证据停止独立收费方向，保留免费工具。周期完成不证明商业成功。

2026-10-02 的展示工作在隔离副本中进行了人工授权的界面精修、完整中英翻译、独立启动整理和实际浏览器验证。手机结果保留尺寸、格式与体积；原检查规则和判定逻辑保持，英文 CSV 只翻译生成的说明，不改用户文件名。截图使用明确命名的合成图片，不是真实客户验收、收入或采用证据。原始运行与私密证据未被改写或复制到公开目录。

Image Checklist was delivered by a real Auto Company model run with five recorded cycles: three exploration cycles and two product cycles. The run retained it as a free local utility after shelving the paid direction. Completed cycles do not demonstrate commercial success.

The October 2, 2026 showcase pass added human-directed visual refinement, complete Chinese/English UI and CSV translation, a standalone local server and actual browser verification. Screenshots use synthetic images. Original model records remain unchanged; the showcase does not claim customer adoption, revenue or commercial validation.
